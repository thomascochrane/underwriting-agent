# Memo structure and house style

Use this user-approved standard for the factual preliminary memorandum after engine-result and supplied-document analysis. Target five pages, with a hard six-page limit including Sources and review scope. The six sections below are required; their analytical content adapts to the asset class. No recommendation is inherited from the example. This standard does not authorize changes to credit policy or engine assumptions.

## Reader-facing structure

Begin with the kicker `Preliminary credit memorandum · [date]` and a plain title. Use full month names in rendered dates. Then include these six section headings in this order:

1. Summary: one factual paragraph covering the proposed transaction, parties, collateral, key results and as-of dates, followed by the few material findings and limitations the reader needs first. Include coverage and loan-to-value where applicable. No recommendation line, investment rating or forced balance of four strengths and four challenges. No shaded callout boxes.
2. Transaction overview: a short introduction naming the source terms document and any inconsistencies, then a terms table with Borrower, Guarantors, Facility, Tenor, Pricing, Use of proceeds and its source, Collateral, Coverage / LTV, Repayment sources, Documentation, and Arranger. Where supplied, summarize sources and uses and the existing/proposed capital structure so the financing purpose is concrete. Reconcile amounts or retain discrepancies. Distinguish proposed from agreed/executed terms. Mark absent or inapplicable facts explicitly.
3. Collateral and portfolio analysis: selected engine metrics, relevant performance chart and concentration/ownership exhibit. State population, period, methodology and material warnings. Explain documented risks and existing protections factually. Listed-security transactions can use price and holders exhibits; other classes use supported substitutes as below. Do not reproduce the whole engine workbook.
4. Borrower, originator and sponsor: business model, relevant parties and roles, dated assets under management where applicable, ownership/control, summary financials and liquidity, and material note disclosures. Identify statement periods and whether statements are management-prepared, compiled, reviewed or audited, including qualifications. Explain material financial movements rather than merely restating the table. Keep guarantor resources separate from borrower liquidity and identify restrictions on support. Include servicing facts where relevant, including dependence on the originator and available reporting. Retain restrictions and adverse findings. Use an ownership chart only when it adds clarity within the page budget.
5. Repayment and structure: contractual repayment sources and their demonstrated availability, cash waterfall, security/guarantees, priority, reserves, covenant terms and legal dependencies. Trace cash from the obligor or collateral through collection, servicing and payment to the financing entity; state timing mismatches and restrictions supported by evidence. Distinguish collateral valuation, contractual security and demonstrated cash availability. Label forecast coverage separately from historical coverage. Include a supported recovery/downside exhibit when applicable and within scope, with assumptions visibly separated from observations. State material unresolved evidence and what cannot be quantified. No recommendation, counter-proposal, approval decision or proposed funding conditions. Describe supplied/existing terms, not what Zivoe should negotiate.
6. Sources and review scope: compact hyperlinked source list with dates and locators as needed, what materials were examined, important missing evidence and calculation assumptions, plus a line identifying exactly which figures are Zivoe estimates (or that none are). Reference retained document versions; links alone are not immutable evidence. Keep detailed logs in supporting records while disclosing material gaps here and beside affected findings.

Keep all six sections. An inapplicable topic or absent document needs a brief factual explanation, not an invented exhibit or a silently omitted section. These headings do not require a new page each.

Number exhibits sequentially, Exhibit 1, Exhibit 2, and so on. Every exhibit has a Source line identifying evidence and as-of dates. An estimate additionally identifies its assumptions/calculation record. Keep material limitations adjacent to affected findings and a compact review-coverage/as-of disclosure in Summary or Sources. Keep the full coverage table and issues ledger in companion records.

A working six-page budget is approximately half a page for Summary, one for terms, one and a half for collateral/engine findings, one for parties/financials, one for repayment/structure, and half a page for Sources and scope. Reallocate to fit the asset class; these are not mandatory page breaks. A shorter complete memo is preferable to padding to five pages.

## Voice

- Use credit-analyst language. No journalistic framing, AI filler, rhetorical contrasts, aphorisms or headline-style bullet titles.
- No em dashes anywhere in the memo, including tables, exhibits, chart labels and repeating elements.
- Define the Borrower, the Guarantors, the Arranger and the sponsor's short name on first use; use the same terms thereafter. The lender is Zivoe. Prefer neutral attribution and factual prose without we; no recommendation section is required or permitted by default.
- Spell out acronyms in labels: average daily trading volume, average remaining lease term, price to book. LTV, AUM, SPAC, LP, FY, USD and SEC are permissible once defined.
- Attribute each counterparty/advisor assertion, for example per the Arranger or per the Borrower's counsel. Preserve short quotations that explain material balances. A sourced claim is not automatically verified. Identify calculations and the status of their inputs. Include illustrative scenarios only when requested or supplied and relevant, with assumptions explicit. Do not replace unknown pricing with a hypothetical rate in a factual terms table.
- Define every constructed ratio, its numerator, denominator and perimeter, for example member notes plus proposed facility excluding non-recourse debt, not a consolidated ratio. Do not give an aggregate ratio a broader name than its inputs support.

## Listed-security collateral module

Use only when realizable market-traded collateral is relevant. Retain exact price, volume, float and holdings figures, currencies, source dates and volume windows. Distinguish stated volume from independently corroborated observations; reconcile split-adjusted prices and units where applicable. The following recovery convention is inherited from the reference workflow and applies only if such a scenario is supplied or requested for the current deal; it is not an observed fact or a universal required exhibit.

The recovery exhibit concerns principal only. Model low, current stated average and high daily-volume cases in one exhibit, with a stated basis for each case. Assume Zivoe sells no more than 25% of daily trading volume and no price impact. Label both as scenario assumptions, not demonstrated execution capacity or legal permission. Sales begin only after default and required enforcement/legal steps. Do not compare the modeled selling period with the facility tenor.

For a constant-price, constant-volume case: daily shares sold = daily market shares traded multiplied by the selected participation rate (at most 25%); daily gross proceeds = daily shares sold multiplied by price; trading days to recover principal = principal exposure divided by daily gross proceeds, rounded up for whole-day presentation. Cap cumulative shares sold at actually available enforceable collateral. If holdings are insufficient, report a principal shortfall rather than a recovery time implying additional shares exist. State currencies and any sourced foreign-exchange rate, gross/net basis, and excluded costs. Never treat no-price-impact or ignored costs as a downside stress. Show coverage and LTV using the same explicitly defined collateral value and exposure. Do not fabricate low/high cases when the evidence cannot support a basis.

Sourced transfer restrictions, lock-ups, possession/control, enforcement, affiliate status and applicable legal sale limits may constrain or invalidate modeled availability. Describe those dependencies. Do not present any fixed Rule 144 volume allowance as settled without transaction-specific current authority and relevant facts. Flag legal confirmation needed; do not issue a separate legal opinion.

## Other asset classes, including MCA

Preserve the memo structure and core analytical purposes. Replace listed-share price and holders exhibits with asset-appropriate performance, collateral composition and concentration exhibits. Record why substituted exhibits are applicable; do not use stock float, stock-sale participation or securities-rule assumptions for receivables.

For MCA, use the installed MCA skills and the actual engine report. Show supplied-population scope, collection/default definitions, eligibility and borrowing-base assumptions, repayment availability and any in-scope sourced collection/recovery scenarios. Do not portray snapshot-inferred defaults as observed losses or renewal credits as cash. Where a material recovery analysis lacks inputs/methodology, state what cannot be established and why rather than inventing recoveries or adding an empty decorative exhibit. Do not add new asset-class calculation methods to the engine through memo prose.

## Word layout and delivery

- Target five pages; six pages maximum, including Sources and all memo exhibits. Preserve readable text and selected core exhibits. After every content or layout change to a Word revision, render again, count pages and inspect all pages for clipping, table breaks, chart/source legibility and letterhead overlap. Word may run longer than LibreOffice, so leave a few lines of slack on the last page and identify the renderer actually checked. Do not shrink type excessively or append extra memo pages to evade the cap.
- Use the bundled assets/zivoe-letterhead.jpg on every page, as a floating full-page image behind content in the repeating header. Follow [letterhead.md](letterhead.md) for exact asset identity, page geometry and safe text margins. Do not substitute an inline image that displaces the memo, a recreated logo or a plain unbranded page.
- Footer: `Internal · Draft · Current as of [date] (prices as of [date]) · Contains information received under NDA · Page n`. Use an actual dynamic page field. Populate true dates. For a transaction without market prices, replace the parenthetical with `prices not applicable`; do not invent a price date. Where price dates differ, show the relevant dates explicitly.
- Trim repeated points first, especially points made more than twice; then remove filler mitigants; then shorten prose and consolidate redundant exhibits. Retain the most material exhibits, adverse evidence and limitations. Detailed engine outputs, calculations, council records and lender questions remain separate supporting files, linked where useful, rather than appended memo pages. Summarize each material supporting finding in the memo so the reader need not discover a hidden caveat elsewhere.
- Preserve numbered prior revisions. Keep the source/evidence, calculation, council and rendering records linked to the exact delivered revision. The final handoff separately identifies any unavailable letterhead, renderer, sources or review roles; do not silently call an unfinished document complete.
