# Memo structure and house style

Use this standard for the preliminary memorandum. It is a presentation standard, not an underwriting policy or authorization to change engine assumptions.

## Reader-facing structure

1. Kicker: `Preliminary credit memorandum · [date]`, followed by a plain title. Use full month names in all rendered dates.
2. Summary: one paragraph covering request, collateral, coverage and loan-to-value, guarantors, recommendation, and two or three reasons. Follow with two columns, Credit strengths / Credit challenges, targeting four evidenced one-line entries in each. If four cannot be supported, use fewer and record why; do not fabricate. Follow with a one-line Recommendation between thin rules. No shaded callout boxes.
3. Section 1, Transaction overview: a short introduction naming the source terms document and any inconsistencies, then a terms table with Borrower, Guarantors, Facility, Tenor, Pricing, Use of proceeds and its source, Collateral, Coverage / LTV, Repayment sources, Documentation, and Arranger. Mark absent or inapplicable facts explicitly.
4. Section 2, Collateral analysis: relevant chart, key metrics exhibit, ownership/holders or concentration exhibit, Collateral strengths, and Collateral risks. Add a Mitigant line only when an evidenced mitigant exists. Listed-security transactions use a price chart and holders table; other classes use meaningful sourced substitutes as below.
5. Section 3, Sponsor and Borrower analysis: identity and role, dated assets under management where relevant, ownership chart, summary financials exhibit, borrower analysis, then Key credit risks and mitigants. Retain liquidity, restrictions and financial-note findings even when unfavorable.
6. Section 4, Recommendation: Decision / Counter-proposal table, recovery exhibit, and numbered counter-proposal conditions. Conditions address collateral, guarantees, maintenance coverage, structure, a lock-up permitting sales whose proceeds prepay the loan, and legal mechanics where relevant. Explain transaction-specific inapplicability rather than copying irrelevant conditions. No exact recommended facility amount or margin-call trigger level.
7. Sources: compact hyperlinked source list with locators as needed, plus a line identifying exactly which figures are Zivoe estimates (or that none are). Reference retained document versions; links alone are not immutable evidence.

Number exhibits sequentially, Exhibit 1, Exhibit 2, and so on. Every exhibit has a Source line identifying evidence and as-of dates. An estimate additionally identifies its assumptions/calculation record. Retain a compact coverage disclosure within the relevant sections; keep the full coverage table and issues ledger in companion records to respect the requested memo structure.

## Voice

- Use credit-analyst language. No journalistic framing, AI filler, rhetorical contrasts, aphorisms or headline-style bullet titles.
- No em dashes anywhere in the memo, including tables, exhibits, chart labels and repeating elements.
- Define the Borrower, the Guarantors, the Arranger and the sponsor's short name on first use; use the same terms thereafter. The lender is Zivoe. Use we only in the Summary and Recommendation.
- Spell out acronyms in labels: average daily trading volume, average remaining lease term, price to book. LTV, AUM, SPAC, LP, FY, USD and SEC are permissible once defined.
- Attribute each counterparty/advisor assertion, for example per the Arranger or per the Borrower's counsel. Preserve short quotations that explain material balances. Label illustrative assumptions, estimates and inferences explicitly, including when pricing has not been proposed.
- Define every constructed ratio, its numerator, denominator and perimeter, for example member notes plus proposed facility excluding non-recourse debt, not a consolidated ratio. Do not give an aggregate ratio a broader name than its inputs support.

## Listed-security collateral module

Use only when realizable market-traded collateral is relevant. Retain exact price, volume, float and holdings figures, currencies, source dates and volume windows. Distinguish stated volume from independently corroborated observations; reconcile split-adjusted prices and units where applicable.

The recovery exhibit concerns principal only. Model low, current stated average and high daily-volume cases in one exhibit, with a stated basis for each case. Assume Zivoe sells no more than 25% of daily trading volume and no price impact. Label both as scenario assumptions, not demonstrated execution capacity or legal permission. Sales begin only after default and required enforcement/legal steps. Do not compare the modeled selling period with the facility tenor.

For a constant-price, constant-volume case: daily shares sold = daily market shares traded multiplied by the selected participation rate (at most 25%); daily gross proceeds = daily shares sold multiplied by price; trading days to recover principal = principal exposure divided by daily gross proceeds, rounded up for whole-day presentation. Cap cumulative shares sold at actually available enforceable collateral. If holdings are insufficient, report a principal shortfall rather than a recovery time implying additional shares exist. State currencies and any sourced foreign-exchange rate, gross/net basis, and excluded costs. Never treat no-price-impact or ignored costs as a downside stress. Show coverage and LTV using the same explicitly defined collateral value and exposure. Do not fabricate low/high cases when the evidence cannot support a basis.

Sourced transfer restrictions, lock-ups, possession/control, enforcement, affiliate status and applicable legal sale limits may constrain or invalidate modeled availability. Describe those dependencies. Do not present any fixed Rule 144 volume allowance as settled without transaction-specific current authority and relevant facts. Flag legal confirmation needed; do not issue a separate legal opinion.

## Other asset classes, including MCA

Preserve the memo structure and core analytical purposes. Replace listed-share price and holders exhibits with asset-appropriate performance, collateral composition and concentration exhibits. Record why substituted exhibits are applicable; do not use stock float, stock-sale participation or securities-rule assumptions for receivables.

For MCA, use the installed MCA skills and the actual engine report. Show supplied-population scope, collection/default definitions, eligibility and borrowing-base assumptions, repayment availability and a sourced collection/recovery downside where supported. Do not portray snapshot-inferred defaults as observed losses or renewal credits as cash. Without the inputs/methodology for a recovery exhibit, show the missing evidence and decision consequence in the exhibit instead of inventing recoveries. Do not add new asset-class calculation methods to the engine through memo prose.

## Word layout and delivery

- Eight pages maximum, including Sources. Preserve readable text and core exhibits. After every content or layout change to a Word revision, render again, count pages and inspect all pages for clipping, table breaks, chart/source legibility and letterhead overlap. Word may run longer than LibreOffice, so leave a few lines of slack on the last page and identify the renderer actually checked.
- Use the supplied letterhead as a floating full-page image behind content. Keep text within its clear content area. Do not substitute an inline image that displaces the memo or a recreated logo.
- Footer: `Internal · Draft · Current as of [date] (prices as of [date]) · Contains information received under NDA · Page n`. Use an actual dynamic page field. Populate true dates. For a transaction without market prices, replace the parenthetical with `prices not applicable`; do not invent a price date. Where price dates differ, show the relevant dates explicitly.
- Trim repeated points first, especially points made more than twice; then remove filler mitigants; then shorten bullets. Do not cut core exhibits, material adverse evidence or limitations to meet the page limit.
- Preserve numbered prior revisions. Keep the source/evidence, calculation, council and rendering records linked to the exact delivered revision. The final handoff separately identifies any unavailable letterhead, renderer, sources or review roles; do not silently call an unfinished document complete.
