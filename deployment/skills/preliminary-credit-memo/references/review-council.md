# Separate-agent review council

Run this when producing the memo, after a source-grounded draft and before final delivery. It is not satisfied by five headings written by the drafting agent. Use available independent agent/delegation tools, checking their real interfaces; no particular tool name is assumed. Reviewer capacity may require sequential batches. Do not modify global agent limits or unrelated deployments to obtain concurrency.

Give each reviewer the same numbered draft, exact engine run and AI-review status, relevant full sources and calculation records, the coverage/issues records, and the user's standing constraints. Include the five-page target and hard six-page limit, factual-only scope, no investment recommendation, decision/counter-proposal or suggested funding conditions, no em dashes, no Open questions/What changed memo sections, no separate legal opinion, conditional asset-class modules and all explicit later refinements. Do not suggest reintroducing a recommendation just because the sample includes one. Settled editorial choices are constraints, not issues to re-raise. New material adverse evidence must still be reported.

Use five distinct roles:

| Role | Review scope |
| --- | --- |
| Credit officer | Accuracy and completeness of repayment, collateral, liquidity and protection findings; omitted adverse evidence and unsupported credit judgments. Do not provide an investment recommendation. |
| Securities or lending lawyer | Choose expertise applicable to the transaction; identify conditional legal mechanics, source limitations, enforceability dependencies, transfer/sale restrictions and drafting overclaims. No separate legal opinion. |
| Numbers auditor | Independently recompute every derived figure from source inputs, including ratios, chart values, coverage, LTV, recovery cases, rounding and unit/currency conversions. Enumerate figures not reproducible. |
| Editor | Factual analyst voice, defined terms, attribution, first-use acronym definitions, full-month dates, em dashes, exhibit/source labels, repetition, six-page cap and removal of recommendation language. |
| Skeptical head-of-fund reader | Challenge whether the factual presentation omits adverse evidence, hides assumptions, overstates verification or nudges an investment decision without support. |

Require each finding to identify a draft locator, issue, materiality, supporting source/calculation or missing evidence, proposed correction, and uncertainty. Reviewers should work independently before the drafting agent reconciles findings. Neither an additional model nor a majority vote makes an unsupported claim true.

Keep council-findings.md alongside the versioned review records, outside the memo. For every finding record reviewer, finding, applicable / not applicable, reason, evidence checked, disposition, and affected revision. Apply applicable findings. List findings awaiting evidence as applicable but unresolved when appropriate, retaining their decision impact. If a reviewer provides a new figure, rederive or corroborate it; otherwise list it to Thor as unverified. Do the same for material figures supported only by one secondary source.

After corrections, recheck affected calculations and claims, obtain focused rereview where a material concern changed, and render/count/inspect the final Word revision. Report the council findings and dispositions to Thor separately; no What changed section goes inside the memo. Preserve objections that remain unresolved.

If the runtime lacks independent agents, sources or sufficient tools for a role, state which review was not completed and why. A self-check may help a working draft but must be labeled as such. Do not claim the five-agent council ran or that figures were independently audited without evidence of those executions.
