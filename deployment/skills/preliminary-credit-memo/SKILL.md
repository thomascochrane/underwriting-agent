# Preliminary credit memorandum

Produce a factual preliminary credit memorandum after Hermes has received and analyzed the underwriting engine's results and reviewed the other supplied deal materials. Target five pages; six pages is the absolute maximum, including exhibits and Sources. Deliver Word on supplied Zivoe letterhead, with a web version only when asked. The Jaguar / LPA memo is a rough presentation reference, not a required recommendation template or evidence for another deal.

The default deliverable contains sourced facts, reproduced calculations and explicitly identified analytical limitations. Do not give an investment recommendation, approve/decline verdict, counter-proposal, suggested facility size, new funding conditions or margin-call triggers. A recommendation requires a separate explicit user request; never infer it from the words credit memo or underwriting. Factual risk reporting and evidence gaps remain required.

## When to Use

- Draft or revise a Zivoe preliminary credit memorandum, including a request for a Word memo, letterhead, or this house style.
- Use credit-memo-drafting for shared evidence discipline. This skill's factual-only scope and six-page cap override that skill's generic recommendation language. Preserve internal issue classifications without turning them into reader-facing approval conditions. This skill does not change credit policy or authorize an investment.
- Apply general standards across asset classes. Use listed-security exhibits only where the actual collateral supports them; do not impose share trading-volume or Rule 144 assumptions on MCA or other receivables.

## Procedure

1. Read every supplied source document in full before drafting: proposed or expected terms, all financial statements and every note, legal memoranda, cap tables, and arranger emails and attachments. Reconcile page counts, appendices and extraction coverage with the source register. For spreadsheets, inspect every relevant sheet, definitions and notes and use reproducible whole-population checks, not selected rows alone. A prior summary, search snippet or engine report is not a replacement for source review. If something is unreadable or missing, record the gap and seek the material; deliver a limited working draft only if the user requests one, visibly stating its coverage and consequences.
2. Read financial notes line by line. Look for going-concern/liquidity disclosures, related-party balances and the explanation of amounts due from affiliates, cash-restricting distribution waterfalls, member/insider notes, investor defaults, and expired commitments. Quote the note that explains a balance, with a locator; do not speculate about its composition. Test whether each repayment source is actually available to the Borrower, including restricted distributions and reimbursements that are not fee income.
3. Check terms for internal inconsistencies, including currency, lender names and carryover text. Surface material inconsistencies early in the Summary and Transaction overview. Attribute management, arranger and advisor claims. Verify current-world assertions such as closings, filings, assets under management and ownership against dated primary sources; retain the verification evidence. If current verification is unavailable, say so and assess the consequence instead of implying it occurred.
4. Build reproducible exhibit calculations and a claim-to-evidence checklist. Record exact market figures, source, observation window and as-of date; never replace those with a rounded typical-day estimate. Define constructed ratios and their entity/debt perimeter. Show coverage and loan-to-value together where applicable, using consistent valuations, dates and exposure definitions. Separate reported facts, corroborated facts and reproduced calculations. Include scenarios only where requested or supplied and relevant, clearly identifying assumptions; never present them as observed outcomes. Inspect the exact engine job, run report, mapping provenance, warnings and actual AI-review status before using its output. Reconcile material differences between the tape results, statements and other documents, or retain the discrepancy explicitly.
5. Use the six user-approved sections in memo-standard.md: Summary; Transaction overview; Collateral and portfolio analysis; Borrower, originator and sponsor; Repayment and structure; Sources and review scope. Keep all six; explain inapplicability or absent evidence within the relevant section instead of silently dropping it. Retain detailed coverage and issue records alongside the memo, and integrate material limitations into the affected factual sections and Summary. No Recommendation, Decision / Counter-proposal, Open questions or What changed section belongs in the default memo. Lender questions and council findings remain separate companion records.
6. State what the evidence establishes and what cannot be established, with the reason and affected metric or repayment source. Describe documented protections, contractual conditions and their actual limits without prescribing new protections or conditions to make the deal acceptable. Sourced proposed facility amounts and covenant thresholds can appear as facts, clearly distinguished from agreed/executed terms; they are not Hermes recommendations. Avoid subjective investment labels such as attractive, acceptable risk or proceed subject to. A source or engine model's favorable opinion is not a factual conclusion for Hermes to adopt.
7. Run the separate-agent review council described in review-council.md, with the draft, source evidence and Thor's standing constraints. Check reviewer claims against sources and calculations. Record each finding as applicable or not applicable with a reason; apply applicable findings and report remaining unverified items separately to Thor. A reviewer assertion is not new verified evidence. If independent agents are unavailable, report the council as incomplete; a single agent's role-playing does not count as separate reviewers.
8. Generate the numbered Word revision on the supplied letterhead, aiming for five pages and never exceeding six pages including exhibits and Sources. Render it, inspect every page, and repeat rendering and page-count checks after every content or layout change to a Word revision, including council corrections. Leave a few lines of slack on the last page because Microsoft Word may paginate longer than LibreOffice. Without a renderer, do not claim the page limit or visual checks passed. Follow the trimming order in the standard, retaining material findings and selected core exhibits; detailed engine workbooks and audit records are separate supporting files, not an appended way around the memo page cap.
9. Preserve every prior version. Save a new numbered file such as preliminary-credit-memo_01.docx, _02.docx, using the next unused number. On Thor's computer, use his actual accessible Downloads directory as requested, retaining the evidence linkage. In the deployed Linux container, save under the deal's versioned review/output directory in /workspace and return the file through the authorized conversation; do not claim it was saved to Windows Downloads or mount the host to achieve that. Record the difference in the handoff. Optional web output follows the same claims, dates, revision and confidentiality markings.

## Pitfalls

- Do not import a prior deal's assumptions or legal conclusions. The listed-security recovery convention is a conditional scenario, not a liquidity guarantee or general receivables method.
- Legal mechanics remain conditional on the sourced facts and required steps. Do not state a fixed Rule 144 sale-volume allowance as certain. The legal reviewer flags mechanics and dependencies; a separate legal opinion is out of scope unless Thor requests it.
- No invented strength, market exhibit, filler mitigant or favorable conclusion to satisfy a layout. Do not impose fixed counts of positive and negative findings.
- No em dashes in memo prose, tables, chart labels, headers or footers. Do not silently alter a verbatim quotation to remove one; select a faithful shorter quote or paraphrase with attribution instead.
- The intended sequence is engine results plus full supplied-document analysis, then memo synthesis and council review. Completion of an engine job alone is insufficient. The current notifier does not automatically start a Hermes turn: this skill defines the handoff requirements, not an implemented automatic callback. Neither skill installation nor a generated DOCX establishes rendered or model-tested behavior.
